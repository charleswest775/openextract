    Questions

    Asked all the time.

    Something else? Ask on GitHub or from the Help button in the app. Stuck? See fixes for common problems.

    My phone is broken. Can I still get my things back?

    Yes. OpenExtract doesn't need the phone, only a backup on a computer. Any backup from Finder, iTunes or Apple Devices will do, even one that's years old. No backup yet? Plug in the iPhone and OpenExtract makes one.

    I only back up to iCloud. Does it work?

    Yes, if you have the phone. Plug it into your computer once and OpenExtract makes a backup there, then opens it. It can't open backups stored in iCloud.

    Does it work with encrypted backups?

    Yes, if you know the password. Encrypted backups are usually the better kind: they also hold Safari history and other things a plain backup leaves out.

    Is it really free? What's the catch?

    Yes, with no strings attached. Every feature is free: no trial, no limit on what you can save, and nothing to upgrade to. If it helped you, pay what it's worth to you, but only if you can. Nothing changes either way.

    What can I save, and as what?

    Texts and WhatsApp chats as PDF, web page, spreadsheet or plain text (here's how to export WhatsApp chats). Photos and videos as the originals. Contacts as vCard or CSV. Voicemails as audio. Notes as text or PDF. Calls as a spreadsheet. Podcast subscriptions as OPML, which most podcast apps import (how to move your podcasts).
