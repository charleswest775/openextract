While it downloads

  1. Install it with your package manager: sudo apt install ./openextract_*.deb
  2. Run openextract or open it from your app launcher.
  3. No backup yet? Plug your iPhone in, open Finder (or iTunes on Windows), and click "Back up now." How it works walks through the rest.

Every download comes straight from the GitHub release, where you can also read the changelog and the source. OpenExtract runs entirely on your computer and never uploads your backup.
