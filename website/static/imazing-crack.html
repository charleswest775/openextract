iMazing crack or free license key?

There's no safe one. A cracked copy of iMazing is a program changed by someone you'll never meet, and you'd be letting it read your entire iPhone backup. If what you need is your data, there are legal ways to get it that cost little or nothing.

We make OpenExtract, a free app for reading iPhone backups. The facts about iMazing here come from imazing.com, checked on November 19, 2026, and link to the page they came from. One thing we tested ourselves in iMazing's trial. It's all listed under Sources.

Why a crack is the worst place to cut corners

To do its job, an app like iMazing reads everything in your iPhone backup: your messages, photos, contacts and call history. If the backup is encrypted, it also holds your saved passwords, such as Wi-Fi passwords and website logins.

With the real iMazing, you're trusting DigiDNA, the company that makes it. With a cracked copy, you're trusting whoever took the license check out. Whatever else they changed, you'd have no way to see it, and it would be running on the files that hold your private life.

A shared or generated license key has a catch of its own. iMazing checks license codes against its own server from time to time, and only codes DigiDNA has on record as active count as valid.

The legal options

iMazing is a good product. If you need what only it does, it's worth paying for. If you only need your data, you may not need to pay at all.

Three legal ways to get your data
iMazing's own free trial.
The trial never expires, and backups for personal use and Quick Transfer are free. What it limits is how much you can copy out: 25 messages, 50 photos, 10 contacts, 5 call-history entries, 5 notes and 1 voicemail. Exporting a whole backup as a folder works in the trial too: we tried it. OpenExtract opens that folder and gets everything out, with no limits. See iMazing's trial limits for the steps.
Buy iMazing.
Its store lists a Personal Subscription at $49.99 a year for up to 5 devices, or $89.99 a year for up to 20. You can cancel any time, and the license stays valid until the renewal date. Students, professors and staff at schools and universities get 30% off. Worth it if you'll use what only iMazing does, such as managing apps and music or moving to a new iPhone.
OpenExtract, free and open source.
If you need the messages, WhatsApp chats, photos, contacts, calls, notes or voicemails in a backup, OpenExtract gets all of them out. Free. No trial, no limits, no account. Every line of its code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read what it does with your backup, each download is built from that code on GitHub's servers, and the Mac and Windows versions are signed. It doesn't manage a phone the way iMazing does.

Questions

Is there a free iMazing license key?

iMazing's store sells only paid subscriptions. The free way to use iMazing is its trial mode, which has no time limit but caps how much you can export. Students, professors and staff at schools and universities get 30% off.

Does OpenExtract need a license key?

No. It's free, with no trial, no limits and no account, so there's nothing to unlock. It's open source under the MIT license.

Will OpenExtract open backups iMazing made?

Yes, after one step in iMazing, and that step works in its free trial. Use Export Backup as Folder, which writes a standard iOS backup folder. Then click Open a backup folder in OpenExtract and pick it.

Sources

We tested one thing ourselves. On November 20, 2026 we installed iMazing 3.6.5 for Windows, chose Use in Trial Mode, and exported a backup with Export Backup as Folder. It didn't ask for a license, and the folder it wrote had every file of the original backup.
