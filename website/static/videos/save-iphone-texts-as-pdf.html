Save iPhone text messages as a PDF

A one-minute recording of OpenExtract, a free app, saving a text conversation from an iPhone backup as a PDF. Each step has a caption, so you can watch with the sound off.

  1. 0:00 Open the backup
  2. 0:16 Find the conversation
  3. 0:33 Save it as a PDF
  4. 0:46 The PDF

What the video shows

  1. Open the backup. OpenExtract's start screen lists the iPhone backups it finds on your computer. Click Open next to yours. If the backup is encrypted, type its password and click Unlock. The password stays on your computer.
  2. Find the conversation. On the backup's home page, click Messages. Every conversation is listed, newest first. Click the one you want; it opens with its photos, dates and times.
  3. Save it as a PDF. Click Save conversation at the top right. Readable document (that's the PDF) is already chosen, so click Save and pick a folder. The PDF is saved there, named after the conversation.
  4. The PDF. It has the whole conversation: every message with its date and time, who sent it, and the photos.

The backup in the video is a made-up one we use for testing, so the messages in it belong to no one. The folder picker is your computer's own window, so it isn't in the recording.
