Why I do it this way

Hi, I'm Charles West. I make OpenExtract, with help from a few contributors.

Most software is priced to get as much as it can, and often that's when people need it most: when a phone has died, when someone they love is gone, when a court date is coming. I don't think that's evil. It's how business usually works. Something useful gets made, and the price is set by what people can be made to pay.

I wanted to try it the other way around. Make something as good as I can, give it to everyone, and trust people to pay what it was worth to them. The people who can afford more cover the people who can't, and nobody is priced out of their own memories.

I don't know yet whether this can keep going. The only way to find out is to try it in the open. If you'd like this way of doing things to exist, the best way to show it works is to pay what it was worth to you.

Thank you, whatever you decide.

— Charles

See the amounts

Not ready yet? This page is always here at openextract.app/support, and there's a “Pay it forward” button at the top of the app.

Stripe takes the payment and emails you a receipt; OpenExtract never sees your card. OpenExtract isn't a charity, so a payment isn't tax-deductible. If you paid by mistake, use the contact details on your receipt and you'll get a refund.

Other ways to help

  • Tell a friend who has an old iPhone backup, or post the link where people ask about iMazing alternatives.
  • Say thanks from inside the app (Settings → Say thanks). It means more than you might think.
  • Like it on AlternativeTo, where people look for alternatives to paid apps, and star it on GitHub. OpenExtract doesn't pay for ads, so this is how the next person finds it.
  • Report a problem from the app (Settings → Report a problem), so it gets fixed for the next person.
