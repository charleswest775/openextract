Hi, I'm Charles West. I make OpenExtract, with help from a few contributors, and I give it away: every feature, to everyone. No trial, no paid tier, and no “pay to export” screen waiting at the end.

That's on purpose. When you're trying to save a parent's voicemails or ten years of texts, the last thing you need is an app that shows you everything, then charges you to keep it.

But free to you isn't free to make. OpenExtract keeps going when the people it helps pay what it was worth to them. Your $5 keeps it free for the next person: someone sitting with an old backup tonight, wondering if their photos are still in there.

Thank you for reading this, whatever you decide.

— Charles

Other ways to help

  • Say thanks from inside the app (Settings → Say thanks). It means more than you might think.
  • Tell a friend who has an old iPhone backup, or post the link where people ask about iMazing alternatives.
  • Star it on GitHub.
  • Report a problem from the app (Settings → Report a problem), so it gets fixed for the next person.
