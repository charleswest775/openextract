Alternative

A free, open-source
alternative to iMazing.

If you just need to get data out of an iPhone backup — messages, photos, contacts, call history — OpenExtract does that part, for free, with source code you can read.

iMazing is a good tool. It's also paid software, closed source, and Windows-and-Mac only. If the only reason you're looking at it is to pull data out of an iPhone backup, you don't need the whole suite. You need an extractor.

That's what OpenExtract is. Nothing else — no music sync, no app management, no live device browsing. Just: point it at a backup, get the data out.

Is iMazing free?

Partly. iMazing's trial mode has no time limit, and a few things are free for personal use: backing up a device, Quick Transfer and app management. Getting your data out is where the limits are. In trial mode iMazing transfers up to 25 messages, 50 photos, 10 contacts, 5 call-history entries, 5 notes and 1 voicemail. Past that you need a paid license, and since June 2025 iMazing sells only yearly subscriptions.

OpenExtract has no caps. Export every conversation, every photo and every voicemail in the backup, as often as you like.

Those limits come from iMazing's own trial FAQ and licensing policy, as of November 2026.

Why people switch
It's actually free.
No trial, no paywall and no cap on how much you export. MIT license, an installer you download once, and it keeps working.
You can read the code.
Every line of OpenExtract is on GitHub. If your workplace or your conscience requires auditable tooling, this is the shape that takes.
Linux.
iMazing is Windows and Mac. OpenExtract runs on Linux too, for anyone on Linux who just wants their data. How to install it on Linux.
No account.
Nothing to register for. Nothing to cancel. Nothing phones home.
Your existing iMazing backups work.
iMazing writes standard Apple backups. OpenExtract reads standard Apple backups. You don't need to re-back-up anything.

Where iMazing still wins

Being honest: if you need more than extraction, iMazing still has advantages.

  • Live device management. Transferring apps, music, ringtones between phones — OpenExtract doesn't do that.
  • Paid support on a deadline. When something breaks on a deadline, a vendor with a support team is different from GitHub issues.
  • Managing the phone itself. iMazing can manage apps, ringtones and live transfers on a connected iPhone. OpenExtract only reads backups.
  • Maturity. Over a decade of edge cases across iOS versions.

See the full comparison for the complete feature matrix. We've also compared OpenExtract with iExplorer, iBackup Viewer and Reincubate's iPhone Backup Extractor.

Switching is easy
Your backups stay where they are.

Install OpenExtract, point it at your existing backup folder, done. You don't need to re-back-up your iPhone or uninstall iMazing.
