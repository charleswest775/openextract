Support

Keep OpenExtract
free for everyone.

OpenExtract is free, and it stays free: every feature, for everyone, with no trial and no paid tier. If it got your data back and you'd like to chip in, you can, in any amount. Nothing in the app changes either way.

Chip in

Pay what it was worth to you, once. The payment page is run by Stripe, and you don't need an account.

Chip in with Stripe →

OpenExtract never sees your card details: Stripe takes the payment and emails you a receipt. OpenExtract isn't a charity, so a payment isn't tax-deductible. If you paid by mistake, use the contact details on your receipt and you'll get a refund.

Where it goes

  • Code signing. Apple's developer program and Windows code signing are what stop your Mac or PC from warning that the app might be unsafe. Both cost money every year.
  • The website. The openextract.app domain, and the service behind the app's feedback form.
  • Time. New kinds of data, fixes for the problems people report, and testing on real iPhones, Macs and Windows PCs.

OpenExtract is made by an independent developer with help from contributors.

Other ways to help

These help just as much as money does:

  • Say thanks from inside the app (Settings → Say thanks). Hearing that it helped is what keeps it going.
  • Tell a friend who has an old iPhone backup, or post the link where people ask about iMazing alternatives.
  • Star it on GitHub.
  • Report a problem from the app (Settings → Report a problem), so it gets fixed for the next person.
