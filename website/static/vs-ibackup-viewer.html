OpenExtract vs iBackup Viewer

iBackup Viewer, from iMacTools, is the closest of the paid tools to OpenExtract: an iPhone backup viewer for Mac and Windows with a free version and a Pro upgrade. Here's how they differ, including where iBackup Viewer is the better pick.

Pick OpenExtract if
  • You want conversations saved as PDF, with their photos, without paying.
  • Your backup is encrypted and you don't want to buy anything to open it.
  • You're on Linux, or want code you can read.
Pick iBackup Viewer if
  • You need WhatsApp, WeChat or Line chats.
  • You need voice memos, calendars or Safari bookmarks.
  • You want to browse the raw files inside the backup.
  • You have an older Mac. iBackup Viewer lists macOS 10.13 or later; OpenExtract's Mac build needs Apple Silicon.

Both read the same Apple backups, so you can try both on yours.

Is iBackup Viewer free?

Partly. According to its product page, the free version extracts contacts, exports and prints messages as text files, exports call history, Safari history and bookmarks, and lets you view and recover photos and videos. Saving iMessages, WhatsApp or WeChat chats as PDF, and what iMacTools calls "many other advanced features", need iBackup Viewer Pro. The iMacTools store lists Pro at $29.95 for a single license, and the FAQ says a license is valid for life, with nothing to renew.

On encrypted backups, iMacTools' own pages disagree. The product page says the free version works with encrypted backups. The FAQ says that since version 2.10, encrypted backups need Pro. If your backup is encrypted, try the free version on it before you buy.

The same FAQ says the Windows version can't save messages as PDF by itself. It prints them instead, and you can print to a PDF printer.

OpenExtract has no Pro version. Encrypted backups, PDF export and everything else are free, on Windows, Mac and Linux.

Answered honestly

Can OpenExtract open the same backups as iBackup Viewer?

Yes. Both read the backups iTunes and Finder save on your computer, and iBackup Viewer's FAQ lists the same backup folders OpenExtract looks in. Encrypted backups need their password in either app.

Does OpenExtract read WhatsApp?

No, not today. It reads Apple's own apps: Messages, Photos, Contacts, Phone, Notes, Voicemail and Safari. If you need WhatsApp, iBackup Viewer reads it.

Can either one put messages back on my iPhone?

No. Both get data out of a backup onto your computer. iMacTools' FAQ says iBackup Viewer can't restore messages to an iPhone, and OpenExtract can't either.

Is OpenExtract really free?

Yes. There's no Pro version, no license and no paid tier. It's open source under the MIT license.
