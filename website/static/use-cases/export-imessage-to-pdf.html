Guide · 3 min

Export iMessage
to PDF.

Open your iPhone backup in OpenExtract (free), click the conversation, and choose PDF. You get the whole thread as a PDF, with photos, dates, times and who sent each message.

PDF export is built in. If your copy of OpenExtract has no PDF option, download the latest from this page first; your backups don't need to change.

01

Make a backup on your computer

Plug the iPhone into your computer with a cable and tap Trust on the phone if it asks. On a Mac, open Finder and click the iPhone in the sidebar. On Windows, open the Apple Devices app or iTunes. Choose to back up to this computer and click Back Up Now. You can tick Encrypt local backup for a fuller backup; just write the password down. OpenExtract can also make the backup for you, from its start screen.

02

Install OpenExtract

Free for Mac (Apple Silicon), Windows 10 and 11, and Linux. Download it here. No account, no trial, no card.

03

Open the backup

On OpenExtract's start screen, choose to open a backup you already have, and pick the backup folder. If the folder holds more than one backup, it shows each one with the device name and date so you can choose. If the backup is encrypted, type the password you set. It stays on your computer.

04

Export the conversation as PDF

Open Messages and click the conversation. Click the save button at the top right (it may be just an icon) and choose the PDF option. If a Save button comes up, click it. Pick a folder. The PDF is saved there, named after the conversation.

05

Or put several conversations in one PDF

Click Select, tick the conversations, then click the button that shows how many you ticked. Choose PDF and the option to put everything in one file, then save. Keep one file per conversation (the default) to get a PDF for each one instead.

What it can't do

  • Only photos that are in the backup. With Messages in iCloud on, your iPhone can keep older photos only in iCloud, so they aren't in the backup. The PDF names those instead of showing them.
  • Pictures, not videos. Videos, voice notes and other files show as a line with their name.
  • Very long threads make very long PDFs. Thousands of messages take a while to export and make a big file.
  • It isn't a certified document. If it's for a legal matter, read printing texts for court first.

Common questions

Does this work on a Mac? Yes. OpenExtract runs on Mac (Apple Silicon), Windows and Linux, and the PDF export works the same on all three.

Will the PDF show dates and times? Yes, but in UTC rather than your local time, so the hour, and sometimes the day, can differ from what the phone showed. Each message has a line like 2026-01-01T09:00:00.594936+00:00 (+00:00 means UTC), and received messages show who sent them.

Can I put several conversations in one PDF? Yes. Click Select, tick them, save them as PDF, and choose to put everything in one file.

Does it work for SMS as well as iMessage? Yes, and for group chats.

Does anything get uploaded? No. OpenExtract reads the backup on your own computer and doesn't send it anywhere. The only thing it asks the internet for is whether a new version is out, and it asks you before it checks. You can turn that off in Settings.

Is it really free? Yes. OpenExtract is open source under the MIT license, with no paid tier, no account and no strings attached. The code is on GitHub.
